OrdinalStatistics.OrdinalStatistics — Module
OrdinalStatistics

Location, variability, association, inequality and poverty measures for ordinal and categorical variables, following the work of R. Peñaloza and J. Souza.

All measures act on an OrdinalDistribution (relative frequencies over m categories ordered from worst to best) and also accept a plain vector of absolute or relative frequencies.
