Location: mean charge, centrum and index

Without a unit of measurement the mean is meaningless, and the median can be ambiguous: for $f = (0.5, 0, 0, 0, 0.5)$ every inner category splits the mass in half. Peñaloza proposes three related location measures, all computed from the frequencies alone.

MeasureTypeRangeFunction
mean charge $\bar S$numerical$[1/m,\ 1]$mean_charge
centrum $\mathcal{E}_\prec$categoricalone category or two consecutive onescentrum
index $\mathcal{I}$numerical$[0,\ 100]$ordinal_index

Mean charge

The post-cumulated frequency (distributional charge) of category $x_j$ is $S_j = \sum_{k \ge j} f_k$, the share of the distribution that did not fall below $x_j$. The mean charge is their average:

\[\bar S = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^m S_j = \sum_{j=1}^m \frac{j}{m}\, f_j .\]

using OrdinalStatistics
f = [5, 5, 50, 60, 80]        # 200 voters rating a candidate's competence
mean_charge(f)
0.805

Centrum

The centrum is the category nearest to $m \bar S$. Here $5 \bar S = 4.025$, so the centrum is the 4th category:

centrum(f)
4:4

It is returned as a range: c:c for one central category, c:c+1 when $m \bar S$ lies exactly half-way between two integers.

centrum([0.5, 0, 0, 0, 0.5]), centrum([0.5, 0, 0, 0.5])
(3:3, 2:3)

Unlike the median, the centrum of $(0.5, 0, 0, 0, 0.5)$ is unambiguous (the middle category), and unlike the mode it uses the whole distribution.

Index

The index rescales the mean charge linearly to $[0, 100]$:

\[\mathcal{I} = 100\,\frac{\bar S - 1/m}{1 - 1/m} = 100 \sum_{j=1}^m \frac{j-1}{m-1}\, f_j .\]

ordinal_index(f)
75.625

Index and centrum agree

The thresholds $t_h = \frac{100}{m-1}\left(h - \tfrac12\right)$ partition $[0, 100]$ into $m$ cells, one per category. The index falls inside the cell of the centrum, and exactly on a threshold when the centrum is a pair.

index_thresholds(5)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 12.5
 37.5
 62.5
 87.5

The voters' index (75.625) lies in $(62.5, 87.5)$, the 4th cell — the centrum found above.

$m$thresholds on $[0, 100]$
325; 75
416.67; 50; 83.33
512.5; 37.5; 62.5; 87.5
78.33; 25; 41.67; 58.33; 75; 91.67

The end cells are half as long as the inner ones: the partition is derived from the index, not imposed a priori.

Reference

OrdinalStatistics.mean_charge — Function
mean_charge(d)

Mean charge S̄ (carga média; also mean post-cumulated frequency):

\[\bar S = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^m S_j = \sum_{j=1}^m \frac{j}{m} f_j, \qquad S_j = \sum_{k=j}^m f_k ,\]

where S_j is the post-cumulated frequency (distributional charge) of category xⱼ. It satisfies 1/m ≤ S̄ ≤ 1, and m·S̄ = k when f is concentrated in x_k.

Source: Souza (1977); Souza & Peñaloza (2005), ch. II; Peñaloza (2012a).

Examples

julia> mean_charge([0.1, 0.5, 0.1, 0.2, 0.1]) ≈ 0.54
true

OrdinalStatistics.centrum — Function
centrum(d; atol = √eps)

Centrum (ordinal expectation) of the observation: the category, or the pair of consecutive categories, nearest to m·S̄. Returned as a range of category positions: c:c for a single central category, c:c+1 when the fractional part of m·S̄ equals ½ (within atol).

\[\mathcal{E}_\prec(X) = \{x_c : c \in \varsigma(m\bar S)\},\qquad \varsigma(y) = \begin{cases} \lfloor y\rfloor, & y-\lfloor y\rfloor < \tfrac12,\\ \{\lfloor y\rfloor, \lfloor y\rfloor+1\}, & y-\lfloor y\rfloor = \tfrac12,\\ \lfloor y\rfloor+1, & y-\lfloor y\rfloor > \tfrac12. \end{cases}\]

Source: Souza & Peñaloza (2005), ch. II; Peñaloza (2012a).

Examples

julia> centrum([0.5, 0, 0, 0, 0.5])
3:3

julia> centrum([0.5, 0, 0, 0.5])
2:3
source
OrdinalStatistics.ordinal_index — Function
ordinal_index(d)

The index ℐ(X) ∈ [0, 100] of an ordinal observation, the linear rescaling of the mean charge to [0, 100]:

\[\mathcal{I}(X) = 100\,\frac{\bar S - 1/m}{1 - 1/m} = 100\sum_{j=1}^m \frac{j-1}{m-1} f_j .\]

It equals the quality index ℋ of Ebserh (2015). The centrum is the category whose cell of the partition given by index_thresholds contains the index.

Source: Peñaloza (2012a); Peñaloza & Macedo (2012).

Examples

julia> ordinal_index([0.1, 0.5, 0.1, 0.2, 0.1]) ≈ 42.5
true
source
OrdinalStatistics.index_thresholds — Function
index_thresholds(m::Integer)

Thresholds t₁ < … < t_{m-1} of the a-posteriori partition of [0, 100] induced by an ordinal variable with m categories:

\[t_h = \frac{100}{m-1}\Bigl(h - \frac12\Bigr),\qquad h = 1,\dots,m-1 .\]

The index lies in (t_{h-1}, t_h) iff the centrum is {x_h} (with t₀ = 0, t_m = 100, end cells closed at 0 and 100), and equals t_h iff the centrum is {x_h, x_{h+1}}. Inner cells have length 100/(m-1); the two end cells have half that length.

Source: Peñaloza (2012a); Peñaloza & Macedo (2012).

Examples

julia> index_thresholds(5)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 12.5
 37.5
 62.5
 87.5
